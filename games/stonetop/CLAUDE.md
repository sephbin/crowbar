# Stonetop sheets

Browser character sheets that look like the paper playbooks (open `index.html`; served at `/games/stonetop/`).

- `index.html`, `stonetop.css`, `stonetop.js`: the app. No build step, no playbook text inside.
- `pack/stonetop-pack.json`: **gitignored**, built from the playbook PDFs. Players load this file once on the home page; it is kept in their browser (localStorage). A pack sitting at `pack/` next to the page is picked up automatically (GM's machine).
- `tools/extract_playbooks.py <folder or zip of "Playbook - The X.pdf"> [out.json]`: needs PyMuPDF. Extracts by region (all nine playbooks share one layout); checkboxes, circles and diamonds are vector drawings found by shape. `tools/show.py pack/stonetop-pack.json heavy moves` prints a playbook's blocks for checking against the PDF.
- `tools/extract_arcana.py <Book II 1-up PDF> [pack.json]`: adds the 18 major arcana (Book II appendix D, front and back page each) to the pack as `arcana`. Run it after `extract_playbooks.py`, which rewrites the pack from scratch. The sheet shows one at `#/arcana/<id>` (Back returns to the sheet it was opened from); any arcanum named in playbook text links there. Ticking such a line adds it to the character (`c.arcana`), and an Arcana tab with the full cards appears while the character holds any.
- Characters live in the browser and can be exported/imported as `stonetop-character` JSON.
- Source PDFs: Andrew's OneDrive `RPG\Stonetop\` (individual playbooks zip; the files can be cloud-only and unreadable), or the combined "Stonetop_Character_playbooks (all)" PDF (18 pages, two per playbook: split it into `Playbook - N.pdf` pairs for the extractor). The playbooks are also in Book I (`~/Downloads/Book_I_..._(1-up)...pdf`, e.g. Silver Tongued p. 111, Impetuous Youth p. 137), which is readable when OneDrive is not. Not yet covered: the playbook inserts PDF (followers, crew, revenant/thrall), the steading and GM playbooks.
- Tabs follow the folded-paper order: Playbook (right half of page 1: background, instinct, appearance, origin), Sheet (page 2), Story (left half of page 1: story prompts and introductions, last), Gear & notes. Saved ticks are keyed by the PDF column, so reordering tabs does not move them.
- Three modes per character (switch in the top bar): Create (stat cards, pick moves and possessions; no rolling, steppers or debilities), Play (taken moves as cards, chosen lines only, rolls, HP/XP, debilities), Progress (internal id levelup; stat +/-, XP and level, all moves with unmet level requirements dimmed). Mode is stored on the character; old characters start in Play.
