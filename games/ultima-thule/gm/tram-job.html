the binding gm ritual not a book spell

Borrowed mechanics: Control's distraction rule (p.60) and the familiar binding (p.56). The daemon becomes the tram's drive and does not fight.

  • Three rounds. Each round is her action (p.124): Perform (Rituals) 75 and 2 PP. Success advances one step. Failure leaves the step where it is and costs 1 PP, as a failed spell does (p.55).
  • Damage or a Dodge (after armour and Protection): Idea roll, INT×5 = 75. Failure loses the current step.
  • Scuffed circle (Agility roll within reach) or focus lifted (Oskar's Sleight of Hand against her Spot 45): the binding collapses.
  • Fumble (00 at 75%): the daemon arrives unbound. It takes the drive anyway, angry.
  • No Control while binding. Control needs concentration and no other spells (p.60).
  • The clue: her reagent focus and the chalk carry the University requisition stamp and the department account code under the Director of Public Engagement.

her opening sequence PP 16

  • Countermagic 2 first, then Protection 2. Countermagic cast onto an existing Protection must beat it on the resistance table (pp.61, 63).
  • Wall 2 across the aisle at the car join: 2 square metres, 30 HP, 15 minutes, 12 m range (pp.64 to 65).
  • Binding: 6 PP across three rounds.
  • Reserve 3 PP for Control 1 on whoever reaches her. That leaves 1 PP; at 0 she falls unconscious (p.55).

imre vs his student p.110 resistance

  • Blast against her Countermagic 2: Blast 1 gets through 45%, Blast 2 50%, Blast 3 55%. A miss rebounds on Imre (p.61). Her Protection does nothing against Blast (p.60); she has no armour.
  • Speak to Mind also has to beat Countermagic 2. Talking aloud does not.
  • She Dispels his spells as a held action: Luck roll, and her Dispel levels must equal or exceed his spell's (p.61). Dispel 35% is her weakest spell.
  • Loyalty: she holds Loyalty (Imre Talvy) 60; he holds Loyalty to her at 60. Either can be called for an inspiration roll (p.215).

verdict threat; TPK risk only if they focus Imre

Four zealots firing Fire 1 at Imre in the same round: Imre's first Dodge out of the circle is 42%, the second 12%, then none (p.125). That averages about 6 damage per round against his 11 HP and 0 AV. Two rounds of focus puts him at 2 HP or less, which is unconscious (p.20).

Against Vesna the same Fire does 1D6 minus AV 3, and Oskar dodges at 80%. Clubs with Sharpen hit at 65% for 1D6+1.

Party output is about 6 to 8 damage per round: Vesna's crossbow fires once every three rounds, then her wrench takes over; Imre has PP for two Blast 2 casts. The zealots run out first: Fire costs 3 PP and their plan leaves each one 2 PP after three casts.

dials p.246

  • Softer: two zealots close to melee instead of casting (the default plan). The fire circle in a crowded car also endangers their own and the passengers.
  • Harder: open with Fire 2 (6 PP, 2D6). That takes Imre down in one focused round. Use it if the party is dominating.
  • Non-lethal end: a zealot who spends to 0 PP falls unconscious (p.55). Fanatics casting to the last point is how most of them drop, which suits "nobody is meant to die".
  • Hazard: Fire on a wooden seat or a coat keeps burning, 1D4 per round to anyone in it (p.62, Fire and Heat p.148).
