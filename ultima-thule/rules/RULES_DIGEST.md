# BRP rules digest (paraphrased)

Page numbers are the book's printed page numbers. The PDF page is printed page + 5.
This digest is a working summary. When a rule matters, confirm it in the PDF text (`rules/pages/`).

## Characteristics (pp.10, 17)
- Rolled: STR, CON, POW, DEX, CHA 3D6; INT and SIZ 2D6+6. Redistribute up to 3 points. Max 21 at creation.
- This edition uses **CHA** (Charisma), not APP.
- Point-buy option (p.17): all start at 10; Normal 24 points, Heroic 36, Epic 48, Superhuman 60. STR/CON/SIZ/CHA cost 1 per point, DEX/INT/POW cost 3. Lowering refunds at the same rate. Min 3 (SIZ and INT min 8).
- Characteristic rolls: ×5 (Effort STR, Stamina CON, Idea INT, Luck POW, Agility DEX, Charm CHA).

## Derived (pp.10, 20)
- HP = (CON+SIZ)/2 rounded up. Major wound = HP/2 rounded up. Unconscious at 2 HP or less; dies at end of next round at 0.
- PP = POW. Recovery 1/hour asleep, 1 per 2 hours awake (POW up to 24).
- Experience bonus = INT/2 rounded up. MOV 10 for humans.
- Damage modifier by STR+SIZ: 2–12 −1D6; 13–16 −1D4; 17–24 none; 25–32 +1D4; 33–40 +1D6; 41–56 +2D6; then +1D6 per 16.
- Full DM on melee and brawl; half (round up) on thrown weapons and bows; none on self-propelled weapons (crossbows, firearms, energy weapons).

## Age (p.10)
- Default 17+1D6. Each full 10 years added: +20/+30/+40 professional points (Normal/Heroic/Epic). From 50: −1 to one of STR, CON, DEX, CHA per decade.

## Skills (pp.11, 33–52)
- Personality type: 13 skills at +20. Brutal, Skilled, Cunning, Charming are listed on p.11; a custom 13-skill set is allowed with GM approval.
- Professional points: Normal 250 (cap 75%), Heroic 325 (cap 90%), Epic 400 (cap 101%), Superhuman 500 (no cap). Spend only on the profession's skills.
- Personal points: INT×10 on any skills (option: INT×15 Heroic, ×20 Epic, ×25 Superhuman).
- Base chances used so far: Appraise 15, Bargain 5, Brawl 25, Climb 40, Command 5, Craft 5, Disguise 1, Dodge DEX×2, Drive 20, Etiquette 5, Fast Talk 5, Fine Manipulation 5, First Aid 30, Gaming INT+POW, Grapple 25, Hide 10, Insight 5, Jump 25, Knowledge 5, Language (Own) INT×5, Listen 25, Medicine 5, Navigate 10, Perform 5, Persuade 15, Pilot 1, Repair 15, Research 25, Ride 5, Science 1, Sense 10, Sleight of Hand 5, Spot 25, Status 15, Stealth 10, Swim 25, Teach 10, Technical 5, Throw 25, Track 10. Weapon skills use the weapon's base.

## Professions used (pp.26–31)
- Soldier: Brawl, Climb, Dodge, First Aid + six of: Artillery, Command, Drive, Firearm, Grapple, Heavy Weapon, Hide, Language (Other), Listen, Jump, Medicine, Melee Weapon, Missile Weapon, Navigate, Repair (Mechanical), Ride, Spot, Stealth, Throw.
- Thief: Appraise, Dodge, Fast Talk, Hide, Stealth + five of: Bargain, Brawl, Climb, Disguise, Fine Manipulation, Firearm (Pistol/Revolver/Shotgun), Grapple, Insight, Listen, Jump, Knowledge (Law), Persuade, Repair (Mechanical), Spot.
- Criminal: Bargain, Hide, Stealth, Drive or Ride + six of: Appraise, Brawl, Climb, Fast Talk, Fine Manipulation, Firearm, Gaming, Grapple, Insight, Jump, Knowledge (Law), Listen, Martial Arts, Melee Weapon, Persuade, Spot, Throw.
- Wizard: Craft, Insight, Knowledge (Occult), two other Knowledge, Language (Other), Listen, Perform (Rituals), Persuade, Research; can use magic and spend professional points on spells.
- Occultist and Priest are also magic-capable professions (check their lists on pp.26–31 before using them for NPCs).

## Success levels (pp.110–112)
- Special: 1/5 skill, rounded normally (70% gives 14). Critical: 1/20 skill, rounded normally (75% gives 4).
- Fumble: 1/20 of the failure chance; 00 always fumbles. Skills with base 5%+ always succeed on 01–05.
- Difficulty: Easy ×2, Difficult ×½.
- Resistance roll: 50% + (active − passive) × 5.

## Fate points option (p.115)
- Spend power points: 5 PP reroll a percentile roll; 5 PP replace a skill with a Difficult Luck roll; 3 PP ignore 1 damage. Optional extras: 6 PP shift a result one level.

## Magic (pp.54–64)
- Each spell is a skill. Starting spells at INT×1: Normal 4, Heroic 6, Epic 8 (+familiar), Superhuman 10 (+staff, familiar).
- Max spell level ½ INT (round up); memorise ½ INT spells. One spell per round; each level costs 1 DEX rank.
- Failure costs 1 PP; fumble loses all intended PP.
- Non-magicians can know ¼ INT (round down) spell levels.
- Spells used: Blast (3 PP/lvl, 1D6/lvl, 100 m, armour absorbs, dodgeable; Countermagic stops, Protection doesn't), Control (3 PP/lvl per its p.60 entry; the p.59 summary wrongly says 1; POW vs POW, 10 rounds, concentration), Countermagic (1/lvl, 5 min, reflects; works vs Blast and Lightning, not Fire/Frost), Fire (p.62; the p.59 summary calls it Flame) (3/lvl, 1D6 fire in a 1 m circle, 100 m; armour, Protection and Resistance absorb; Countermagic no effect), Illusion (1/lvl, 3 SIZ visual per level, 30 m, 15 min), Perception (1/lvl, nearest named thing, 60 m), Protection (1/lvl, +1 AV/lvl, 15 min), Speak to Mind (1/lvl, one mind per level, 100 m, 10 rounds).
- Wizard's staff: costs about a new car plus 1 permanent POW (p.57).

## Passions (pp.214–216)
- Start: one at 80%, two at 60%. Types: Devotion, Fear, Hate, Honor, Love, Loyalty; also Distrust, Respect, Greed and GM-made ones.
- Inspiration (once per scene): crit +50%, special +30%, success +20%; failure −10% for the scene; fumble −1D10% to the passion and despair.
- 80%+ can force mandatory rolls; oppose with another passion to act against it.

## Weapons and armour (Chapter Eight, pp.169–180)
- Light club: Club 25%, 1D6+dm. Light mace: Mace 25%, 1D6+2+dm, parries. Knife: Dagger 25%, 1D3+1+dm. Quarterstaff: Staff 25%, 1D8+dm, 2H. Fist: Brawl 25%, 1D3+dm.
- Light crossbow 1D6+2, ½ attacks/round, 40 m. Medium 2D4+2, ½, 50 m. Heavy 2D6+2, 1/3, 55 m, STR 13. Repeating 1D6+2, 1/round. Crossbow base 25%.
- Throwing knife: Missile 15%, 1D4+½dm, 20 m, 1/round.
- Armour: Heavy clothing 1, soft leather 1, hard leather 2 (−10% Physical), cuirbouilli 3 (−10% Physical), chain 7 (−20% Physical).

## NPCs (pp.221–246)
- Bestiary entries are built for a Normal campaign. For Heroic games, raise threat with numbers, equipment or armour (p.246: +3 armour already makes a foe challenging; +6 makes it very hard to harm).
- Unlisted NPC skills default to base chances.
