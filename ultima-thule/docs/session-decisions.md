# Decisions from the claude.ai session (7 to 8 Oct 2026)

- System: BRP over PF1e for this group (players asked for D&D; expectation of combat).
- Power level Heroic; passions on; no hit locations; no strike ranks; fate points via PP.
- Adventure: crew hired via the black market to deal with pro-magic anti-tech extremists. University secretly funds them; mercenaries make it look like it is dealing with the problem.
- Imre is the University's liaison, sent by the Director of Public Engagement (his doctoral-cohort rival). Being sent is part punishment, part rivalry.
- Extremists target a driverless tram and bind a daemon to replace its drive intelligence. The daemon ends up as the tram's drive, not a combatant.
- Three routes through the runaway tram: Vesna repairs the drive, Oskar reaches the manual brake, Imre talks to the daemon (Speak to Mind + Persuade).
- Dispel removed from Imre for Speak to Mind. Countermagic kept; it does nothing against the zealots' Fire (p.62; the p.59 summary calls it Flame).
- Energy weapons and their ammo are rare in Thule: Vesna has a heavy crossbow and heavy wrench, Oskar throwing knives.
- Oskar rewritten from smooth talker to dexterous thief (Thief profession, custom Nimble personality).
- Player sheets: Synth style only, Cutive Mono headings, tabs, tap-for-details skill modal with success grades, fake redactions, Story tab. No spoilers.
