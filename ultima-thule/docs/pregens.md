# Tram Job pregens (BRP Universal Game Engine, Heroic)

Build: point-buy at Heroic (36 points, DEX/INT/POW cost 3), personality type (13 skills +20), 325 professional points (cap 90%), +30 professional points per full 10 years added to rolled age, INT x10 personal points. Passions RAW: one at 80%, two at 60%. Fate points option: spend power points (5 PP reroll, 3 PP per damage point ignored). All builds verified by `tools/brp_check.py`.
Setting note: energy weapons and especially their ammo are rare in Thule, so the party uses crossbows and thrown weapons.
